## Slide 2 - The Problem (In Plain Language)

India has **roughly 1 million ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist) and ANM workers** acting as the first point of contact for rural healthcare - covering pregnant women, infants, the elderly, and chronic disease patients across villages with little or no internet connectivity.

The problem is **not a lack of data**. India already collects enormous amounts of health data through government systems. The real problem is this:

- A health worker manually decides, every single day, **which house to visit first** - with no help, no ranking, no warning system.
- A high-risk pregnancy and a routine check-up look identical on paper until it's too late.
- Referrals to hospitals happen on paper or phone calls, with **no tracking, no follow-through, no accountability**.
- Doctors at the hospital end have **zero visibility** into which villages or patients need urgent attention until the patient physically arrives - often too late.

**In short: India has digitized the paperwork of rural healthcare, but not the decision-making.**

---

## Slide 3 - Why This Matters (Human Stakes)

- Maternal and infant mortality in rural India remains disproportionately driven by **delayed identification** of risk, not lack of medical knowledge.
- ASHA workers are responsible for hundreds of households each, with no triage tool beyond memory and instinct.
- Specialist doctors are concentrated in cities; India's doctor-to-patient ratio in rural belts is far below global norms, making **prioritization of who sees a doctor first** a life-or-death operational question.

VitaWeave's premise: **if you can't fix the doctor shortage overnight, you can fix who gets seen first.**

## Slide 4 - Existing Solutions & Why They Fall Short

| Platform | What it does | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **ANMOL / RCH Portal** | Government data entry & reporting tool for ANMs | Pure reporting; no prioritization or decision support |
| **CommCare** | Digital forms platform used by NGOs | A form-builder, not an intelligence layer |
| **eSanjeevani** | National telemedicine (doctor-to-doctor / doctor-to-patient video consults) | Has supported over 282 million consultations with standardised data capture and AI-generated diagnostic recommendations, and has crossed 43 crore (430 million) total consultations - but it is a **call/video infrastructure layer**, not a frontline triage or care-coordination system. Independent research has flagged real adoption gaps: awareness of teleconsultation services in rural communities was found to be as low as 2.2%, and clinical reviewers note sub-optimal integration of general practitioners within the tele-referral pathway, inadequate training of health-workers, and a lack of feedback loops.

**The common thread:** every existing platform answers *"how do we record/connect/call?"* - none of them answer *"who needs help most, right now?"*

This is the white space VitaWeave occupies.

## Slide 5 - The VitaWeave Solution

**VitaWeave is not another health-records app. It is a decision-support and care-orchestration layer that sits on top of (and works alongside) existing systems.**

It is built as **one mobile app, three roles, one shared intelligence brain**:

### 🧑‍⚕️ ASHA / ANM Worker App
Daily AI-prioritized task list, patient registration, community health alerts, vaccination tracking, AI assistant for quick guidance - works **offline-first**.

### 👩‍⚕️ Doctor Dashboard
A live queue of patients ranked by urgency (not by arrival time), AI-assisted diagnostic support, telemedicine interface, population-level health campaign tools.

### 🧍 Patient App
Personal vitals, medication reminders, appointment tracking, and a simple AI health chat in the patient's own language.

**The connecting thread:** an **AI Urgency Scoring Engine** that continuously looks at patient data (vitals, visit history, risk factors) and tells the health worker *exactly who to see next and why* - instead of leaving that decision to memory and instinct.

---

## Slide 6 - How It Actually Works (Simple Walkthrough)

1. **Register** - ASHA worker adds a patient's basic profile, even with no internet (data is saved locally and syncs automatically once connectivity returns).
2. **Score** - The system looks at the patient's data (e.g., a pregnant woman with swelling and high blood pressure) and automatically flags her as high-risk.
3. **Prioritize** - That patient now jumps to the top of the ASHA worker's task list for that day, instead of being just one more name on a long list.
4. **Refer** - If escalation is needed, VitaWeave routes the case toward the right facility or telemedicine specialist and **tracks whether the referral was actually completed** - closing a loop that today simply does not exist.
5. **Coordinate** - The doctor on the other end sees this patient already flagged, with context, instead of starting from zero.
6. **Learn** - Every outcome feeds back into the system, gradually improving how cases are prioritized over time.

## Slide 8 - Market Opportunity

- India has approximately **1 million+ ASHA/ANM workers** and an even larger base of Auxiliary Nurse Midwives, PHC staff, and rural health volunteers - every one of them is a potential daily user.
- The Indian government's own infrastructure shows the scale of demand: eSanjeevani alone has now crossed 43 crore (430 million) total consultations, with over 131,000 health centres acting as spokes connected to 16,900+ hospital-based hubs - proving that government will fund and scale digital health infrastructure when it works.
- India's national digital health backbone, the **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)**, is actively expanding interoperable health records, creating a ready-made integration path for any new platform that adds genuine value rather than duplicating existing data.
- Beyond India: similar frontline-worker shortages and triage gaps exist across Sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia, and Latin America - meaning the underlying engine (not the India-specific UI) is globally exportable.

**Bottom line: this is not a "new market to create" - it is a large, government-funded, already-digitizing market that currently lacks an intelligence layer.**

## Slide 11 - Current Build Status (Honest Snapshot)

**What already exists and works (Functional MVP):**
- Full role-based app (ASHA/ANM, Doctor, Patient) built on React Native/Expo - works on both Android and iOS from one codebase.
- Supabase backend with authentication, real-time data sync, and offline caching/sync-queue system designed for low-connectivity villages.
- Google Gemini AI integration for: AI-generated daily task prioritization for ASHA workers, an AI medical assistant persona ("MedGemma") for quick guidance, and AI-assisted diagnostic support for doctors.
- Voice interface foundation (working on web, with regional Indian languages planned).
- Complete screen sets for all three roles: patient registration, community alerts, vaccination tracking, appointment queues, telemedicine UI, patient vitals/medication tracking, and health chat.

**What is still needed before real-world deployment (Use of Funds priority):**
- Deploying and hardening the production backend schema with a real district/state pilot's data.
- Replacing the simulated mobile voice recognition with a production-grade speech engine for regional languages.
- Building a real (not UI-only) telemedicine video backend.
- A full clinical and data-security/privacy review (critical for any health data product in India, given DPDP Act compliance).
- Structured QA, monitoring, and error-reporting infrastructure.

**This transparency is intentional** - VitaWeave is presented honestly as a working prototype with proven architecture, not an unfinished idea, and not an inflated claim of production readiness.

## Slide 13 - Technology Stack (For Technical Diligence)

- **Frontend:** React Native + Expo (cross-platform mobile), Expo Router for role-based navigation
- **Backend:** Supabase (auth, Postgres database, real-time subscriptions)
- **Offline Layer:** AsyncStorage-based local cache + custom sync queue with conflict resolution
- **AI Layer:** Google Gemini API for chat, diagnostic support, and structured task generation
- **Voice:** Web Speech API (working) with a mobile-native speech engine planned for regional languages
- **Maps/Location:** Google Maps APIs for field worker routing
- **Future-state additions:** blockchain-backed health identity layer (already scoped in design), predictive ML risk models, ABDM/ABHA interoperability

## Slide 14 - Impact Goals

- Reduce average time between a high-risk case being identifiable and a health worker actually visiting that patient.
- Reduce referral "drop-off" - the number of recommended hospital referrals that never actually happen.
- Increase the number of high-priority cases an ASHA worker can correctly identify per week.
- Provide district health authorities with a live, honest view of where care gaps exist - not just a count of forms filled.

Framed against the national baseline: even the country's flagship telemedicine platform admits structural gaps - sub-optimal integration of general practitioners within the tele-referral pathway, inadequate training of health-workers, the absence of mechanisms for re-referrals, and a lack of feedback loops. VitaWeave is designed specifically to close exactly these gaps rather than duplicate the call-routing function eSanjeevani already does well.

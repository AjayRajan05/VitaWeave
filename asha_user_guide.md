# 🏥 VitaWeave: ASHA Worker User Guide

Welcome to **VitaWeave**! This guide will help you navigate the application to better manage your community's health, track patient records, and access AI-powered health guidance.

---

## 🔑 1. Getting Started: How to Login
1. **Open the App:** Select the **ASHA/ANM Portal** on the login screen.
2. **Enter Phone Number:** Type your registered 10-digit phone number.
3. **Verify OTP:** You will receive a 6-digit code via SMS. Enter it to log in.
    > [!TIP]
    > For demo purposes, you can use:
    > - **Phone:** `+91 77777 77777`
    > - **OTP:** `123456`
    > - Or simply click **"Skip for now"** to enter Demo Mode.

---

## 📊 2. Your Dashboard: Daily Overview
The Dashboard is your home screen. It provides a real-time summary of your work:
- **Community Health Signals:** A color-coded banner showing the current risk level of your ward.
- **Quick Stats:** Track your total patients, active health campaigns, and work hours.
- **Weekly Alerts:** Stay updated on missed vaccinations or critical follow-ups.
- **Today's Tasks:** A checklist of visits and activities you need to complete today.
- **Health Tips:** Quick reminders about pregnancy care, nutrition, and sanitation.

---

## 👥 3. Managing Patients (CRM)
Click the **Patients** icon in the bottom navigation bar:
- **Search & Filter:** Find patients by name or filter them by **High, Medium, or Low Risk**.
- **⚡ Auto-Triage:** Click this button to let the AI automatically prioritize your patients based on the urgency of their health condition.
- **Patient Details:** Click on any patient to see:
    - **✨ Clinical Summary:** An AI-generated summary of their health history.
    - **🏥 Smart Referrals:** Recommended government services or hospital check-ups.
    - **📞 Quick Call:** Use the green phone icon to call your patient immediately.
- **Add Patients:** Use the **(+)** button (coming soon) to register new members of your community.

---

## 🤖 4. AI Assistant & Insights
Click the **AI Assistant** icon for intelligent guidance:
- **💬 Chat Mode:** Ask questions like "What is the vaccination schedule for infants?" or "Tips for pregnancy nutrition."
- **📊 Insights Mode:** View AI-detected patterns in your ward, such as a sudden rise in fever cases or clusters of missed prenatal check-ups.
    > [!IMPORTANT]
    > To enable the most advanced medical logic, you can set up a **Gemini API Key** in the settings (Key icon 🔑).

---

## 🛠️ 5. Service Nexus
The **Services** tab is your toolbox for community health:
- **📅 Campaign Planner:** Plan and manage health awareness drives.
- **🚨 Emergency Hub:** Quick access to the PHC ambulance (108) and your supervisor.
- **📜 Government Schemes:** Check eligibility for schemes like **PM-JAY** and **Janani Suraksha**.
- **🩺 Telemedicine:** Book video or phone consultations for your patients with doctors.
- **💉 Vaccination Tracker:** Follow the immunisation schedule for your village.

---

## 📡 6. Community Signals
The **Signals** tab helps you monitor broader health trends:
- **Anomaly Alerts:** Instant warnings about disease outbreaks.
- **💊 Pharmacy Trends:** Monitor which medicines are being bought most frequently in your ward (e.g., rising sales of Paracetamol may indicate a fever outbreak).
- **📈 Weekly Case Trend:** A graph showing the total reported health cases over the past 7 days.

---

> [!NOTE]
> VitaWeave is designed to work in areas with poor internet connection. Most features will save your data locally and sync when you are back online.
